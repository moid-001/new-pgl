A. General Service Questions

What services does Perfect Gulf Limited offer?
We provide 7 core engineering services across Saudi Arabia: Electrical engineering layouts, commercial HVAC climate systems, NFPA-certified Fire Protection sprinklers, Low Current building automation (BMS), UPS backup power redundancy, White Space data center setups, and commercial Plumbing/Drainage grids.
Which industries do you serve?
PGL serves 10 core industrial sectors: Manufacturing factories, institutional/commercial offices, T3/T4 Data Centers, EV networks, solar renewable farms, healthcare medical facilities, smart residential complexes, temporary mobile setups, logistic warehouses, and municipality/government complexes.
What geographic areas do you cover?
Our physical office is located in Dammam, Eastern Province, KSA, and we actively serve industrial megaprojects throughout the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. We also retain design facilities in Keswick, Ontario through our parent company, ARRHAM Group, Canada.

B. Project & Process Questions

How do you ensure project timelines are met?
PGL utilizes a structured 5-step methodology (Assessment, Scheduling, CAD Design, Commissioning, Handover). We coordinate schedules directly with B2B operators to determine shifts (including low-occupancy night shifts), minimizing daily workflow disruption.
Do you handle municipality permits & civil defense approvals?
Yes. PGL drafts all engineering single-line plans, fire suppressions layouts, and cooling blueprints to comply fully with Saudi Building Codes and civil defense criteria, handling local regulatory clearances on behalf of the client.

C. Technical & Capability Questions

Are your technicians and engineers certified?
Absolutely. Our engineering leads and installation crew hold credentials matching local municipality licenses, SBC, NFPA standards, and international IEEE distribution guidelines.
What standards do you comply with?
All electrical layouts meet IEC & IEEE rules; HVAC duct designs are optimized for hot climates; fire safety installations strictly adhere to civil-defense and NFPA rules; and plumbing layouts comply fully with sanitary specifications.

We perform physical on-site assessments and cooling load analysis, review client architectural briefs, and deliver itemized transparent quotes covering engineering labor, materials procurement, municipal clearances, testing, and handover documentation.

Yes. PGL offers preventive maintenance agreements covering active HVAC chiller tuning, panel load checks, generator testing, and data closet calibration.
