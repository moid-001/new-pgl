Service-Specific FAQs

Are your CCTV and security designs HCIS compliant?
Yes. For industrial plants, utility sites, and infrastructure megaprojects in Saudi Arabia, we design and install physical security systems matching High Commission for Industrial Security (HCIS) directives, including Class I/II perimeter controls, HCIS-certified IP cameras, and 90-day storage servers.
What are the benefits of a Building Management System (BMS)?
A BMS acts as a central brain, monitoring and controlling HVAC, lighting, electrical panels, and fire systems. By using intelligent setback schedules and tracking energy consumption dynamically, a BMS typically reduces commercial electricity bills by 15% to 25%.
Do you provide test reports for structured network cabling?
Yes, absolutely. For every Cat6A or fiber run installed, PGL provides detailed Fluke DTX/DSX test reports. These documents verify link attenuation, near-end crosstalk (NEXT), and splice integrity, guaranteeing gigabit network performance.
