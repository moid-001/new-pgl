Design Services

  • Electrical Load List & Load Flow Analysis: Accurate mapping of power requirements and load behavior to ensure balanced system design.
  • Short Circuit & Arc Flash Studies: Safety analysis to protect equipment and personnel, ensuring compliance with NFPA and IEC standards.
  • Single Line Diagrams: Simplified representation of the electrical system for easy understanding and future maintenance.
  • MV & LV Distribution Network Design: Structured design of medium and low voltage networks, including generators and automatic transfer switches (ATS).
  • Power Cable Schedules & Termination Plans: Comprehensive documentation of cable runs, terminations, and connectivity for accurate installation.
  • Lighting Layouts & Controls: Design of energy efficient plant and area lighting, including automated lighting control systems.
  • Grounding & Lightning Protection: Systematic grounding layout and surge protection for equipment safety.
  • Fire Alarm & Low Current System Layouts: Design schematics for integrated life safety and low voltage systems.
  • Process Control Panel & Instrumentation Drawings: Customized design for industrial control panels and instrumentation wiring.

Implementation Services

  • MV & LV Switchgear Installation: Setup and configuration of switchgears to manage power flow and ensure safe operation.
  • Power Transformers & Overhead Transmission: Installation of step-down transformers and high-voltage gantry systems.
  • Cable Tray & Conduit Works: Routing of cables using AL ladder-type trays, underground (U/G) and above-ground (A/G) conduit systems.

  • Power & Lighting Panel Installation: Configuration of main distribution boards, sub-panels, and lighting panels.

  • Grounding & Lightning Systems: Earth pit construction, bonding, and surge protection setup.
  • Solar Power Plant Installation: End-to-end installation and connection of photovoltaic systems for renewable power.

Service-Specific FAQs

Do you compile single-line diagrams for SEC clearance?
Yes. Our design engineering division compiles certified Single-Line Diagrams (SLD), load balance schedules, and earth testing reports conforming strictly to Saudi Electricity Company (SEC) and local municipality requirements.
Can you handle high-load substation equipment installation?
Absolutely. We handle utility transformer rigging, medium-voltage cable splicing, switchgear assembly, power factor correction panel setups, and grounding grid layouts under complete engineering supervision.
What testing do you perform before project handover?
PGL performs detailed pre-commissioning tests including insulation resistance testing (Megger), loop impedance testing, phase rotation analysis, ground-grid soil resistance tests, and active load bank testing under supervision.
