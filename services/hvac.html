Service-Specific FAQs

Do you perform cooling load calculations before recommending equipment?
Yes. We perform comprehensive thermal analyses using Carrier's Hourly Analysis Program (HAP) to determine the exact peak BTU cooling demand, factoring in wall insulation values, glass solar gain, lighting load, and expected human occupancy.
Are your duct installations compliant with SMACNA?
Absolutely. All supply, return, and exhaust duct networks are fabricated in our facility or on-site to match SMACNA specifications. We perform physical smoke or light pressure leak-testing on all seams before insulation.
Can you integrate HVAC equipment with existing Building Management Systems?
Yes. We integrate all HVAC chillers, VRF panels, and AHUs directly with central Building Management Systems (BMS) using open industry protocols like BACnet, Modbus, or LonWorks.
