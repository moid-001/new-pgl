ISO-Compliant Data Center Cleaning Services

Data centers are highly sensitive environments where even microscopic airborne particulates can cause electrostatic discharges, cooling inefficiencies, and catastrophic hardware failures. Our specialized cleaning division utilizes advanced anti-static equipment and strict containment protocols to deliver pristine, sterile environments.

Our Comprehensive Cleaning Protocol Includes:

  • Subfloor Void Decontamination: Eliminating heavy concrete dust, construction debris, and zinc whiskers from under the raised access floor to optimize air supply pressure.
  • Sub-System and Rack Detailing: Removing surface particulates from outer server casings, cable runways, patch panels, and server blanking plates using specialized electrostatic discharge (ESD) safe micro-fiber materials.
  • Ceiling Plenum Cleaning: Decontaminating overhead cable trays, return air plenums, and lighting fixtures to prevent overhead gravity-fall dust deposition.
  • ISO 14644-1 Standard Alignment: Standardizing operations to achieve certified Class 8 (or better) airborne particulate counts, ensuring reliable airflow and optimal thermal dissipation.

Service-Specific FAQs

What is the difference between data center "White Space" and "Grey Space"?
White Space represents the actual server room footprint containing server racks, cable runs, containment doors, and local cooling units. Grey Space contains the heavy support infrastructure—like diesel generators, primary chillers, substation rooms, and fuel yards—that keep the White Space running.
Do you align layouts with Uptime Institute Tier classifications?
Yes. Our design engineering division models layouts, A/B dual power busways, and In-Row cooling alignments to conform fully with Uptime Institute Tier III (Concurrently Maintainable) and Tier IV (Fault Tolerant) site requirements.
Why is Aisle Containment important in a server room?
Aisle Containment isolates cold air supply paths from hot server exhaust paths. By preventing this air from mixing, the cooling system operates much more efficiently, eliminating hot spots and allowing the data center to support high-density server loads (over 10kW per cabinet).
