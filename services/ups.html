Service-Specific FAQs

How do you size a standby diesel generator?
We perform active on-site electrical monitoring or review design parameters to determine the peak continuous kW load. We factor in high-transient currents, such as heavy HVAC motor starts, sizing the generator's alternator to accept these transient spikes without voltage dips.
What is an Automated Transfer Switch (ATS), and why is it needed?
An ATS is an electromechanical switch connected to both utility power and your standby generator. When it senses a grid drop, it signals the generator to start. Once the generator reaches the correct voltage, the ATS safely disconnects the utility grid and connects the generator load—all within seconds.
Do you offer preventive maintenance for battery arrays?
Yes, absolutely. We offer customized preventive maintenance agreements. Our technicians perform thermal imaging checks on battery terminals, test individual cell voltages under load, check electrolyte levels, and replace aging batteries to prevent emergency drops.
