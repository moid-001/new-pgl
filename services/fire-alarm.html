Service-Specific FAQs

Do you assist in obtaining KSA Civil Defense approvals?
Yes. Perfect Gulf Limited handles the entire regulatory approval process. We compile certified fire safety layouts, hydraulic calculations, and equipment specifications, submitting them directly to Civil Defense authorities on your behalf.
What is a clean-agent fire suppression system, and where is it used?
Clean agent systems (like FM-200 or Novec 1230) use dry, non-conductive gases to suppress fires by heat absorption without leaving chemical residues or water damage. They are mandatory for critical B2B locations like server rooms, data centers, and switchgear closets.
What is the difference between wet and dry pipe sprinkler systems?
Wet pipe networks are filled with pressurized water, discharging instantly when a sprinkler nozzle detects high heat. Dry pipe networks are pressurized with nitrogen or air, holding water back until an alarm triggers. Dry networks are ideal for unheated spaces or critical storage areas to prevent water damage from leaks.
